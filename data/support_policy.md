# Flipkart Support Policy (Sample)

This is a short, original sample policy document written for this project's
support-assistant demo. It is NOT Flipkart's real policy.

## Apparel and Footwear Returns
Apparel and footwear items are eligible for return or exchange within 7 days
of delivery, provided the item is unused, unwashed, and has original tags
attached.

## Electronics Returns
Smartphones and laptops follow a replacement-only policy for manufacturing
defects reported within 10 days. Cash refunds are only issued if no
replacement unit is available.

## Home and Kitchen Returns
Home and kitchen products can be returned within 10 days of delivery if they
are in original packaging with all accessories included.

## Cash on Delivery (COD) Refunds
Refunds for COD orders are processed via bank transfer (IMPS/NEFT) and take
2 to 3 business days to reach the customer's account after approval.

## Prepaid Order Refunds
Refunds for prepaid orders (card, UPI, wallet) are credited back to the
original payment method within 24 to 48 hours of approval.

## Standard Delivery Timelines
Metro areas typically receive orders within 2 to 3 business days. Other
regions may take 4 to 7 business days depending on serviceability.

## Reverse Pickup
Reverse pickup is free of charge for items that are damaged, defective, or
incorrect. For non-serviceable pin codes, customers may need to self-ship the
item and will be reimbursed for shipping costs.

## Order Cancellation
Orders can be cancelled free of charge any time before they are shipped.
Once shipped, the customer must either refuse delivery or file a return
after the order arrives.

## Refurbished Product Warranty
Refurbished products carry a 6-month seller warranty and can be returned
within 7 days of delivery if found defective.
